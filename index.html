Help & safety

PainLocator helps you record where pain occurs, track how it changes, and share a clear summary with your clinician.

Privacy

  • Entries are patient-entered and stored in this browser unless you export or share a file.
  • Demo data is fictional and kept separate from your real entries.
  • Do not put identifying medical details into Feedback.
  • PainLocator is not marketed as HIPAA compliant in this client-only configuration.

Medical boundaries

  • This app is not a diagnostic service and does not replace professional medical evaluation.
  • Automated summaries are observations about your recorded entries only.

PainLocator is not for emergencies. Seek immediate medical care for severe or sudden symptoms.

Developer / demo

Experience mode is for development and demos. Production routing should choose Patient or Clinician from role or session context — patients should not pick a product mode.
